Digital & AI Engineering

Digital engineering is where our sensor data becomes a decision. We build the artificial intelligence, machine learning, and big-data analytics software that reads, interprets, and acts on signals from mechanical and electromagnetic sensors.

Focus areas

Core research & engineering programs

We treat software as part of the sensor: the model that interprets a signal is designed alongside the hardware that produces it.

Artificial intelligence

Machine Learning for Sensor Data

Models trained on mechanical, acoustic, and RF sensor data to classify events, detect anomalies, and predict degradation ahead of failure.

Data infrastructure

Big Data Analytics

Pipelines that ingest, clean, and analyze high-rate sensor streams at scale, turning raw time-series data into structured, queryable insight.

Embedded & edge

Sensing Software

Firmware and edge software that runs signal processing and inference close to the sensor, reducing latency and bandwidth needs.

Systems integration

Digital Engineering
