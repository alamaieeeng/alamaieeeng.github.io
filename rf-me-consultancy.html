03 · Applied Engineering

RF & ME Consultancy

We take RF and mechanical engineering programs from concept through validated design — design, Ansys-based simulation, thermal analysis, and physical testing, delivered as a single engagement or as focused support to your team.

Engagement model

How a project moves through our shop

Design

RF and mechanical concept development, requirements definition, and architecture trade studies.

Simulate

Ansys-based electromagnetic, structural, and thermal simulation to validate performance before hardware is cut.

Test

Prototype build-up and lab testing to confirm the simulated performance holds in the physical part.

Validate

Results, reports, and design recommendations handed back to your team, ready for the next design cycle.

Services

What we support

Engage us for a full program or drop us in where your team needs an extra set of hands and tools.

RF Design

Antenna, RF front-end, and link-budget design for sensing, communication, and power-transfer applications.

Mechanical Design

Structural and mechanical design for sensors, enclosures, and mounting systems, built for manufacturability.

Ansys Simulation Support

Electromagnetic (HFSS-class) and structural finite-element simulation support — model setup, meshing, solving, and results review for your Ansys workflow.

Thermal Simulation

Thermal analysis of RF and electronic assemblies — heat dissipation, hot-spot identification, and thermal management design.

Prototyping

Rapid prototyping of RF and mechanical assemblies to move from simulated design to a testable physical unit.

Testing & Validation

Bench and environmental testing to characterize RF performance, mechanical response, and thermal behavior against spec.
