Why SimuSolv

Research depth, consultancy speed.

Cross-domain by design

Our mechanical, RF, and computational teams work from the same models, so a sensor concept, its power budget, and its data pipeline are designed together, not handed off.

Simulation-first

Ansys-based electromagnetic, mechanical, and thermal simulation lets us validate a design before it's built, shortening the path from concept to prototype.

Research-grade, field-ready

We publish and prototype at the research frontier, then translate that work into designs, reports, and datasets our partners can act on.
