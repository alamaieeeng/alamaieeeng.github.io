About Us

Engineering that moves between disciplines.

SimuSolv was founded on a simple observation: the hardest engineering problems in sensing sit at the boundary between disciplines — where a mechanical structure meets an electromagnetic field, and where a raw signal has to become a usable decision. We work across mechanical sensor research, RF and electromagnetic engineering, applied simulation and testing, and digital and AI engineering, so a project never has to be handed between disconnected teams.

How we work

What guides the engineering

Physics first

We start from first-principles device physics — mechanical, electromagnetic, and thermal — before reaching for a simulation tool or an off-the-shelf model.

Simulation-validated

Ansys-based electromagnetic, structural, and thermal simulation is part of the design process, not an afterthought before testing.

Built to be used

Research and consultancy work is delivered as something a partner can act on: a validated design, a report, a dataset, or a working prototype.
